I’ve been on the PPC strategy team at a performance marketing agency for the last few years. The book covers ecom, B2B SaaS, and lead gen across roughly thirty active client accounts. The thread that brought me to bidding automation specifically: it’s the most consequential single decision in any PPC stack, and the most marketed-against by vendors making claims they can’t back up. Someone has to do the diligence.

Why a directory, not a listicle

Listicles get stale the moment they’re published. A directory with a fixed rubric, a quarterly refresh, and a public changelog stays useful. The rating tags, the comparison matrix, the ML-approach classification — all rerun every three months.

What I won’t do

Sponsored placements, affiliate-for-ranking deals — declined.
